  2. This storybook is better than the one I read last week.
  3. Where were you going yesterday at five o'clock...?
  4. My family has lived in this neighbourhood for five years.

Section D: Transformation (4 Marks)

  1. She went to sleep early because she was feeling very tired.
  2. All the exam papers were graded by the teacher yesterday.
  3. Although it was raining heavily, the students went out for outdoor training.
  4. Sarah said that she was studying for her English exam.

Section E: Extension (3 Marks Rubric)

  • Q19 (1 Mark): Appropriate use of modal 'should' for recommendation and 'must' for strong requirement.
  • Q20 (1 Mark): Coherent 3-sentence narrative correctly contrasting Past Continuous (background action) and Past Simple (interupting action).
